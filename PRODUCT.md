# Product

## Register

product

## Users

B2B SaaS founders and marketing teams at tech companies with 1–100 employees. They are in a workflow — reviewing posts, approving content, checking campaign performance. Primary task on any given screen is making a decision (approve, edit, publish, skip). They are professionals operating under time pressure; the interface should be decisive and never in their way.

## Product Purpose

SOSH is an AI-powered social media management platform. Users define campaigns, the AI generates platform-specific posts in their brand voice, they review and approve, then the platform publishes automatically. Every post is human-approved before publishing. Success looks like: a user going from zero to scheduled posts across LinkedIn and X in under 10 minutes, with content they are proud to put their name on.

## Brand Personality

Confident, precise, unhurried. A tool that respects the user's intelligence — no over-explanation, no hand-holding, no noise. Like a sharp B2B SaaS tool built by people who also use it.

## Anti-references

- Generic SaaS dashboards (blue gradients, hero metrics, cream/sand backgrounds as "warmth")
- Busy social media schedulers cluttered with counters and carousels
- Toy-like UIs with rounded blobs, illustrated empty states, and emoji as UI elements
- Error pages that apologize profusely, show raw stack traces, or use "Oops!" copy

## Design Principles

1. **Precision over decoration.** Every element earns its pixel. The Stone palette carries atmosphere; no decorative chrome needed.
2. **Human-in-the-loop is a feature.** The interface presents AI output for judgment, not celebration. Neutral tone, clear affordances.
3. **Errors are recoverable events.** An error page is a composed, calm moment — not a panic. Reference IDs exist for support; the user should never feel blamed or lost.
4. **Typography carries hierarchy.** Scale and weight contrast do the work; color is used for status and action, not decoration.
5. **Consistency is credibility.** Error states, empty states, and loading states use the same spacing scale, type scale, and radius as every other surface.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.1 AA minimum. Body text must meet 4.5:1 contrast. Interactive elements meet 3:1. The platform is multilingual (en, pt, es); error copy must be available in all three languages without layout breakage. Reduced motion respected via `prefers-reduced-motion`.
