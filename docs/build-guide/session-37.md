# Session 37 — Analytics and the monthly report: the Accountability surface (ADR 0031) · Track O

> **Goal:** ship **T1-B** (`docs/pre-launch-scope.md` §4): an analytics surface at three levels (**post,
> campaign and portfolio**), plus a **monthly report** the founder can forward to a board. The report is
> generated by Jemip and delivered to the business's own members. Forwarding it is the founder's act. The
> session also produces the **Plus/Pro analytics split** as a written definition, so the C-2 pricing copy
> can be written against something that exists (§0 L-6).
>
> **Why now.** T1-B is **half of C-2, the one hard launch gate** (`pre-launch-scope.md` §9, §12.9 P-6).
> Both tiers promise analytics (Plus *"basic analytics"*, Pro *"advanced analytics"*), and
> `docs/product-status.md:128` records that *"Metrics are collected from platforms and stored; nothing
> displays them."* The P-7 ruling (`pre-launch-scope.md` §14) sequenced Track L ahead of it and wrote the
> cost down: *"it spends a session T1-B could have used ... T1-B is still open and unscheduled."* This
> session pays that debt. It is also the cheapest Tier-1 item left: §4 says *"cheap once Session 33
> lands"*, and Session 33 (ADR 0026) has landed.
>
> **What this session does NOT ship, explicitly:** **any new metrics collection, platform read scope or
> change to the day-1/3/7 sync cadence** (LinkedIn metrics stay unavailable, Reality §2; L-2); **the
> engagement inbox** (T1-A, Session 40); **founder / personal profiles** (T1-E, Session 38; this session
> only has to avoid blocking them, L-7); **the content portfolio view** (`docs/ideas.md` §3, `OPEN`; the
> topic/funnel/give-vs-ask mix is a different feature from the portfolio *performance* level built here);
> **growth, reach or virality prediction** (`ideas.md` §3, `PARKED`, which would break the
> attribution-honesty rule); **UTM tagging or conversion-event ingestion**; **follower counts**;
> **cross-brand benchmarks** (*"how you compare with other Jemip customers"*); **any change to ADR 0026's
> normalisation, baseline, floor or promotion rules**; and **sending the report to any address that is not
> a member of the business** (L-5 ⚑).
>
> **Prerequisite, absolute.** Session 37 does not begin until Session 36 has closed **and merged to
> `master`**. That is satisfied: PR #16 merged as `87161b2a`, and PR #18 (the QA defect fixes and the
> A-8 hosted repair) merged on top of it as `2df055a6`. **Soft prerequisites, both still open:** **no
> production OAuth app is registered**, so no real tenant has a single metric (`docs/current-phase.md`);
> and `S34-E2E-UNVERIFIED` / `S36-UX-UNVERIFIED-IN-BROWSER` (`docs/backlog.md`): the generation path and
> the last session's UI have never been exercised end to end in a real browser by a real user. **This
> session is therefore built and tested on seeded data only, and its empty state is the state every
> launch-day customer will actually see (L-4).**

## Reality check — to be re-verified against the live repo before the Architect runs

> Read at `origin/master` `2df055a6` (the PR #18 merge). This guide's own branch, `docs-sessions-37-40`,
> forks at `87161b2a` and differs only by docs and the PR #18 commits. **If any item has changed, correct
> this file before the Architect runs.**

1. **`post_metrics` holds the latest value only. There is no history.** One row per post, *"updated in
   place by the metrics worker via: INSERT ... ON CONFLICT (post_id) DO UPDATE"*
   (`supabase/migrations/20260430120011_post_metrics.sql:3-4`). Its columns are `likes, comments, shares,
   saves, clicks, reach, impressions`, all nullable: *"NULL means the data is unavailable"* (`:6-7`). The
   sync predicate stops at **day 7 plus a 2-day grace** (`20260919100000_metrics_sync_cadence.sql:4-16`,
   `METRICS_MAX_AGE_DAYS` default 9). **So every post's number freezes at roughly day 7, and nothing can
   show a post's curve over time.** A monthly view is therefore an aggregation of frozen per-post values
   grouped by `published_at`, not a time series of one post. **Q1 decides the unit of measurement, and
   whether any trend view needs a history table, which L-2 forbids unless the ADR argues it.**

2. **LinkedIn reads no metrics at all today. Only X does.** `PLATFORM_CONFIGS.linkedin.metricsReadAvailable
   = false`, with the comment *"r_member_social_feed not granted; fetchPostMetrics is NOT_IMPLEMENTED (ADR
   0028 Amd A)"*. `twitter` is `true` (`lib/social/platforms/config.ts:24-39`). The capability is exposed
   as `metricsReadAvailableFor` (`lib/social/index.ts:32`), and the campaign page already renders a
   *"metrics unavailable"* state from it (`lib/outcomes/campaign-view.ts:128-135`). **The Plus tier is
   LinkedIn + X. At launch, half of Plus's platforms produce no analytics.** That is the most important
   fact in this file. **Q1 and Q2 must answer it honestly.** A new read scope is out of scope (L-1), and
   Session 38 is where the OAuth scope set is next opened.

3. **The two platforms are measured on different bases, by design.** ADR 0026 §6.2: X is a **rate**,
   `(likes + comments + shares) / impressions`; LinkedIn is a **count**, `likes + comments + shares`, with
   `impressions` *"permanently null"*. *"LinkedIn's count is a known, uncorrected bias"*, disclosed on
   every LinkedIn surface verbatim: *"LinkedIn results compare engagement counts, which also rise as your
   audience grows."* (`docs/decisions/0026-outcome-loop.md:424-437`). `lib/outcomes/normalise.ts:12-15`:
   *"NULL IS NEVER ZERO ... An X post with impressions = 0 is excluded."* **Any portfolio number that adds
   an X rate to a LinkedIn count is wrong. Q1 owns the rule, and Q6 the disclosure.**

4. **Session 33 already produced most of the analysable data, frozen and immutable.**
   `supabase/migrations/20260919110000_outcome_tables.sql`: **`post_outcomes`** is one write-once row per
   matured post (`value`, `metric_basis`, `baseline`, `baseline_n`, `log_lift`, `beat_baseline`,
   `length_band`, `cta_present`, `hook_survived`), indexed `(business_id, platform, published_at DESC)`
   (`:96-97`). **`post_dimensions`** is one row per AI authorship event (`role`, `format`, `origin_mode`,
   `hook_type`, `proof_type`). **`campaign_retrospectives`** is one verdict per campaign
   (`supported | not_supported | inconclusive`, `n`, `wins`, Wilson interval). All three have SELECT-only
   RLS and REVOKEd writes (`:24-30`). **Human-written posts get no `post_dimensions` row** (`:16-17`), so
   any "by role" or "by hook" breakdown silently covers AI posts only. **Q1 decides which table is the
   source for each number, and Q6 decides how the coverage gap is disclosed.**

5. **A campaign-level learning view already ships, and the authenticated-client rule was learned the hard
   way.** `loadCampaignLearningView` (`lib/outcomes/campaign-view.ts:137-162`) is called once, from
   `app/[locale]/(dashboard)/campaigns/[id]/page.tsx:73`. Its comment: *"`client` MUST be the caller's
   AUTHENTICATED client (never service-role): this is a user-facing read, so the SELECT policies on the
   outcome tables, not the businessId argument alone, are what scope it (MAJOR-1, L-9)."* **Q3 decides
   whether the campaign level of the new surface extends this view, links to it or replaces it. Two
   campaign analytics surfaces that disagree are the failure to design against. SHARED-FUNCTION CALLERS
   applies to it and to `unavailableMetricsPlatforms`.**

6. **The one existing metrics list query is unindexed for its own sort and has a memory caller.**
   `listTopPostMetrics` orders by `likes DESC` (`lib/db/post-metrics.ts:33-46`). The only index is
   `post_metrics_business_id_idx` (`20260430120011_post_metrics.sql:27-28`). Its sole caller is
   `lib/memory/performance.ts:74-77`, which feeds performance memory. **It sorts by a raw count across
   both platforms**, which is exactly the Reality §3 mistake. **Q7 decides whether analytics reuses it (no:
   it mixes bases) and whether its memory caller is left alone (L-2 says yes unless the ADR names the
   change).**

7. **There is no plan-gating abstraction. Plan checks are scattered string comparisons.**
   `business.plan === 'trial'` (`lib/ai/context.ts:99`, `lib/campaigns/enforcement.ts:14`), `=== 'plus'`
   (`enforcement.ts:23`), `=== 'pro'` (`lib/campaigns/generate.ts:328`). `Plan = 'trial' | 'plus' | 'pro'
   | 'agency'` (`lib/db/types.ts:55`), and `PaidPlan = 'plus' | 'pro'` (`lib/stripe/products.ts:10`).
   **Q2 decides where the basic/advanced line is enforced, what a trialist sees, and what `agency` gets.**
   A UI-only gate is not a gate.

8. **There is no report artefact, no report email and no chart or PDF dependency.** `EmailKind` has six
   values (`lib/email/types.ts:1-7`), mirrored by the `email_outbox_kind_check` CHECK (last widened in
   `20260709120000_email_outbox_team_invite_kind.sql:3-7` by drop-and-re-add). Email is
   `@react-email/components` + `@react-email/render` + Resend through the outbox and its drain cron.
   `package.json` has **no charting library and no PDF renderer**. **Q4 decides the artefact's format and
   delivery. A new dependency (chart or PDF) is a founder adjudication** (CLAUDE.md *"Do not add new
   dependencies without confirming"*; L-6 ⚑ proposes none).

9. **Scheduled work runs through QStash, and two outcome-loop schedules are still not created.**
   `vercel.json` is `{}`, and crons are QStash schedules that call `app/api/cron/*`
   (`docs/launch-checklist.md:128-154` at `2df055a6`). `extract-outcomes` (`0 4 * * *`) is *"NOT YET CREATED as a
   QStash schedule"* (`:153`), and so is `backfill` (`:154`). **The monthly report needs data the
   un-scheduled `extract-outcomes` cron produces.** Q4 decides the report's own trigger and must list its
   schedule as a launch-checklist row, not as an assumption.

10. **The AI layer works against the real API again, but only as of `2df055a6`.** `QA-REAL-API-SOSH-FIELD`
    (every `runPrompt` call returned HTTP 400) is fixed on master: *"lib/ai/client.ts's real-client adapter
    strips it"* (`lib/ai/runner.ts:218-221` at `2df055a6`). `docs/backlog.md:24` on master still says the
    fix is *"not yet merged"*, **which is stale: correct it at close-out (§5)**. The daily budget has
    **four** purposes, `('triage_cents', 'generation_posts', 'backfill_cents', 'planner_cents')`
    (`20260922110000_campaign_plan_proposal_rpcs.sql:430`). ADR 0029 A-4 declined a fifth. **If the report
    narrative uses a model, Q5 decides its budget purpose. A fifth purpose is a founder adjudication.**

11. **Governed memory's honesty rules already bind anything that renders a pattern.** The constitution:
    *"A performance pattern is rendered with the number of observations behind it ("based on 7 posts"),
    never as an instruction."* Outcome patterns are read through `lib/memory` (`listOutcomePatterns`, as
    used at `campaign-view.ts:150-151`), never from the table (`MEM-NO-DIRECT-TABLE-ACCESS`). ADR 0026 §8.5
    / `get_learning_cycles_northstar` is **an operator aggregate across brands, by design**, granted to
    `service_role` only (`20260919140000_outcome_rpcs.sql:398-404`, `:438-441`). **It must never reach a
    customer surface. Q7 names the scan that proves it does not.**

12. **Tenancy has the known array trap.** `get_user_business_ids()` returns an **array**, so for a
    multi-business user RLS alone does not isolate one business from another, and `.eq('business_id', …)`
    is the sole boundary (cerebrum, Session 34 K1; ADR 0030 §7). A portfolio query that forgets the `.eq`
    returns a *union* of the user's businesses, and RLS will not catch it. **Q7's Tier-1 plan needs the
    two-businesses-one-user arm and a positive control.**

13. **The UI has two known gaps this session's surface would inherit.** `QA-MINOR-UI` (`docs/backlog.md`):
    the dashboard shell overflows at 320 px, and the sidebar shows the raw key `nav.team`. A new `analytics`
    nav entry lands in that same sidebar. **Q8's UX contract states whether this session fixes the shell
    overflow it inherits (a table or chart at 320 px makes it worse) or explicitly does not.**

## §0 — Locked decisions (binding input — derived from existing rulings; ⚑ items awaiting founder confirmation)

These are decided. The Architect (O1) **encodes** them in ADR 0031 and names their losers; it does **not**
re-open them. Where a Locked decision and this guide disagree, the guide is wrong — flag it. Where the ADR
needs to contradict a Locked decision, it **STOPS and flags for founder adjudication**.

**Where these come from.** Unmarked items restate rulings that already exist: the constitution's Governed
Memory and attribution rules, `pre-launch-scope.md` §4 T1-B and §9 C-2, ADR 0026 §6 and §10.2, ADR 0028
Amendment A, and `ideas.md` §3. **Items marked ⚑ are this guide's proposals.** They follow the nearest
precedent, but no founder has ruled on them yet. **The founder confirms or revises each ⚑ item before §1a
is pasted.** A revision is recorded here with a prime (`L-5` → `L-5′`), and the original stays visible.

**Locked (L):**

- **L-1 — Session 37 ships the Accountability surface and nothing that feeds it.** *In scope:* an
  analytics surface at post, campaign and portfolio level; the monthly report artefact (generation,
  storage, in-app view, delivery to members); the written Plus/Pro analytics split and its server-side
  enforcement; the empty, thin-data and metrics-unavailable states; and the C-2 pricing-copy
  recommendation as text in the ADR. *Out of scope, explicitly:* **new metrics collection, platform read
  scopes or sync cadence**; **the engagement inbox**; **founder profiles**; **the content portfolio
  (mix) view**; **any prediction**; **UTM / conversion ingestion**; **follower counts**; **cross-brand
  benchmarks**; **any change to ADR 0026's normalisation, baseline, floor or promotion rules**; **any new
  memory writer** (analytics reads memory and writes none); and **editing the pricing page or `CLAUDE.md`'s
  pricing line** (the founder applies C-2 after reading the ADR's recommendation). If a step appears to
  need any of these, **STOP and report**.

- **L-2 — Analytics is read-only over measurement that already exists.** The surface and the report read
  `post_metrics`, `post_outcomes`, `post_dimensions`, `campaign_retrospectives` and outcome patterns (via
  `lib/memory`). They write none of them, change no worker, and make **no platform API call**. Any new
  table holds **derived report artefacts only**, never a second copy of a metric. Loser: a metrics
  history table so the surface can draw curves. It changes the collection layer (the X API is billed per
  read, ADR 0028 §14.3), and it is the one design that turns an Accountability session into a
  collection session.

- **L-3 — The honesty rules of governed memory and ADR 0026 govern every number shown.** **NULL is never
  zero**, so an unavailable metric renders as unavailable and never as `0`. **No sum or average crosses
  `metric_basis`**, so an X rate and a LinkedIn count are never added or averaged together. Every pattern
  is shown **with its n** and as an observation, never as an instruction. **Association is never
  presented as causation**, so the copy says "posts with a question hook averaged", not "question hooks
  increase engagement". **The ADR 0026 §10.2 LinkedIn disclosure appears verbatim** wherever a LinkedIn
  count is shown. *"Metrics unavailable"* is read from `metricsReadAvailableFor` and never re-derived from
  a platform name. Loser: a single blended "engagement score" across platforms. It is what every
  competitor shows, and it is false here (Reality §3).

- **L-4 — The empty state is the launch state, and it is designed first, not last.** No real tenant has a
  metric (goal block). LinkedIn reads nothing (Reality §2). A Plus customer on LinkedIn only will see
  **no numbers at all** on day one, and that screen must still be honest, useful and non-alarming. Empty
  (nothing published), immature (published, under 7 days), unavailable (platform cannot read) and thin
  (below the n floor) are **four distinct states** with distinct copy. Loser: a generic "No data yet"
  placeholder. It tells a LinkedIn-only customer that data is coming when it is not.

- **L-5 ⚑ — The report is delivered only to the business's own members. Jemip never sends it to a
  third-party address.** "Forward to a board" is the founder's act from their own inbox, or from a
  download or print. Loser: a "send to my board" field with an arbitrary recipient address. It turns the
  product into a mailer for business data to unverified recipients, adds an abuse and deliverability
  surface to the Resend domain, and needs a consent story for people who are not users. The
  human-in-the-loop principle points the same way: the founder decides what leaves the company.

- **L-6 ⚑ — No new dependency. Charts are server-rendered SVG with Tailwind, and the report's "PDF" is a
  print stylesheet over the in-app report page.** This follows the constitution's dependency rule and
  `CLAUDE.md`'s *"Tailwind only"*. Loser: a charting library (Recharts and similar), which brings client
  bundle weight and a `'use client'` boundary for what are a handful of bar and line marks. Also a loser:
  a server PDF renderer, which brings a new runtime dependency, a font-embedding problem across en/pt/es,
  and a second rendering path to keep in sync with the page. **If O1 finds a requirement only a dependency
  meets, it STOPS and flags it.** That is founder adjudication, not O1's call.

- **L-6′ — REVISED by founder ruling A-2 (2026-10-04); supersedes L-6 above, which stays visible.** A
  **charting library and a server-side PDF renderer are both permitted.** O1 recommends the specific
  packages in ADR 0031 and argues each one. The arguments must cover client bundle weight and where the
  `'use client'` boundary falls (charts stay inside the smallest Client island; the page shell stays a Server
  Component), the PDF renderer's fit on Vercel's Node runtime and its font embedding for en/pt/es, and
  **one source of truth for report content**: the PDF and the in-app page render from the same stored
  snapshot payload, never from two independent queries. The package names are confirmed in §0.2 before O2
  installs them. *Still binding:* `CLAUDE.md`'s *"Tailwind only"* for styling, and the chart text-alternative
  and colour rules of Q8.

- **L-7 — Every analytics read is sliceable by connected social account, not only by platform.** Session
  38 (T1-E) adds personal and founder profiles beside company pages, so one business will have two
  LinkedIn accounts. Grouping by `platform` alone would need a retrofit. Loser: platform-only grouping,
  which is simpler today and wrong in five weeks. O1 states which column makes posts sliceable by account
  today, or that none does and what the smallest honest step is. **That step must not pre-build T1-E.**

- **L-8 — GDPR, tenancy and RLS obligations in full.** Any new business-scoped table (a stored monthly
  report, a report-run log): RLS in the InitPlan-wrapped form, **`USING` and `WITH CHECK`** on every

  the same commit as its migration**, and `purge_business` coverage. Writes go through a service-role
  worker or a `SECURITY DEFINER` RPC granted to `service_role`. **No new member write path.** Every

- **L-9 — Contract discipline + constitution rules, inherited by every step.** Anthropic SDK only via

## §0.1 — Questions the Architect (O1) must resolve IN the ADR (BINDING)

**O1's ADR must decide each one explicitly, name the loser, and tier the resulting constraint** (ADR 0015
§2). Ground every answer in the real seams. Let the single `ecc:code-explorer` sweep map them and cite
`file:line`.

- **Q1 — The metric model: what number is shown, from which table, at which maturity (the load-bearing
  question).** (a) **Per platform**, the headline number and its source. Is it `post_outcomes.value` (frozen,
  day-7, basis-aware, but present only for matured posts in a campaign with an eligible metric) or
  `post_metrics` (latest, raw, present from day 1)? Or both, at different maturities? State what a
  2-day-old post shows. (b) **LinkedIn** (Reality §2): what the surface shows for a platform that reads
  nothing. Say it plainly and argue the loser. (c) **Aggregation**: the period unit (calendar month in which
  timezone, and whose: the business's, the member's, or UTC?), and the rule that keeps rates and counts
  apart (L-3). Is a portfolio X rate a mean of per-post rates or a ratio of sums, and why? (d) **Baseline
  and lift**: does the surface show `beat_baseline` / `log_lift` (ADR 0026 calls `log_lift` *"descriptive
  only"*), and to whom? (e) **Coverage**: AI posts have dimensions and human posts do not (Reality §4).
  State what every dimension breakdown covers, and how the uncovered share is shown. (f) **Whether any
  read needs a new SQL aggregate or RPC, or plain bounded selects suffice.** If an RPC, it is `SECURITY
  INVOKER` or a DEFINER with explicit business scoping, argued against L-8.

- **Q2 — The Plus / Pro split, and where it is enforced.** Write the definition of **"basic analytics"**
  and **"advanced analytics"** as a list of surfaces and numbers, so the C-2 pricing copy can quote it.
  Argue it against what exists, not what would be nice: Reality §2 means that **Plus customers have
  analytics on X only at launch**, and the ADR must say what the pricing copy should therefore claim.
  Then: **where the gate lives** (a single capability function in `lib/` beside the scattered checks of
  Reality §7, or another existing seam); what a **trialist** sees; what **`agency`** sees; and what a
  downgrade from Pro does to a stored Pro-level report. **A UI-only gate is not a gate. Name the
  server-side check and its tier.** End the section with **the C-2 copy recommendation as literal
  en/pt/es strings** for the founder to adopt or revise (L-1: O1 recommends it, the founder applies it).

- **Q3 — Information architecture of the surface.** The route(s), and the post / campaign / portfolio
  levels. How the **campaign level relates to the shipped `loadCampaignLearningView`** (Reality §5):
  extend it, link to it or replace it. Two campaign surfaces that can disagree is the failure to avoid.
  The **portfolio** level: what it groups by (period, account per L-7, platform, campaign, and which
  `post_dimensions` columns), and the **default view** a founder lands on. Where outcome patterns appear
  (with n, L-3), and whether retrospective verdicts appear outside their campaign. **Navigation**: the new
  sidebar entry and its relation to `campaigns` and `opportunities`. **Account slicing** (L-7): the column
  that makes it possible today, or the smallest step that does not pre-build T1-E.

- **Q4 — The monthly report artefact.** (a) **What it is**: an immutable stored snapshot (numbers frozen at
  generation, so the report a founder forwarded in March still says the same thing in June), or a live
  page parameterised by month. Argue the loser against *"forward to a board"*. (b) **When it is generated**:
  the trigger (a QStash cron on day N of the month, in which timezone; Reality §9), its dependency on the
  not-yet-scheduled `extract-outcomes`, what happens when the month's posts are not mature until day 7 of
  the next month, and **idempotency** (one report per business per period, enforced where?). (c)
  **Contents and order**: the sections, the numbers in each, and which are Pro-only (Q2). (d) **Delivery**:
  the in-app view, the email (a new `EmailKind` and the `email_outbox_kind_check` widening, Reality §8),
  which members receive it, opt-out, the email's locale (the recipient's or the business's), and the
  print / PDF path (L-6 ⚑). (e) **The empty report**: does a business with nothing measurable that month
  get a report, a short "nothing to report, here is why" note, or nothing? Argue it against L-4.

- **Q5 — The narrative: model or no model, and how numbers stay true.** Does the report carry prose beyond
  templated sentences? **If yes:** which model through `lib/ai/` (`ecc:cost-aware-llm-pipeline` grounds the
  choice), the `CustomerContext` it receives, and **the number-fidelity rule**: the model receives a
  structured payload of pre-computed numbers and may only restate them, verified after generation by a
  deterministic check that every figure in the prose exists in the payload, so that a hallucinated *"up
  34%"* fails closed. Also: the **injection surface** (post content, campaign names and retrospective
  hypotheses are customer- or model-authored text inside the prompt; name the guard), the **budget
  purpose** (Reality §10: a fifth purpose is a founder adjudication), the **per-report cost ceiling** in
